# SkipQ 🍔

> **Canteen ordering, simplified.**

SkipQ is a comprehensive, dual-interface mobile application designed to modernize and streamline the fast-paced environment of campus or corporate canteens. By bridging the communication gap between hungry customers and busy food stall operators, SkipQ eliminates long physical queues and brings efficiency to daily dining.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🎓 For the Customers (Students)
Designed for speed and convenience, the customer-facing portal allows users to order their favorite meals without ever having to stand in line.
* **Secure Access:** Dedicated login portal ensuring order history and preferences are securely tied to the user's account.
* **Intuitive Browsing:** A clean, easily navigable digital food court featuring diverse options from fast food staples to local favorites.
* **Real-Time Order Tracking:** Live status updates with color-coded tags indicating if an order is `PENDING`, `READY`, or `UNAVAILABLE`.

### 👨‍🍳 For the Vendors
The vendor portal acts as a lightweight, digital Point of Sale (POS) and Kitchen Display System, empowering stall owners to manage their business directly from their mobile devices.
* **Active Order Management:** A real-time command center displaying incoming orders. Vendors can update workflows instantly with `Prepare`, `Ready`, or `N/A` buttons.
* **Dynamic Menu Control:** Keep offerings up-to-date by instantly adding new items and prices, or deleting discontinued ones.
* **Sales & History Tracking:** A comprehensive log of past orders to monitor daily performance, review specific transactions, and track total revenue.
* **Stall Customization:** Full administrative control to rename the storefront or manage platform presence.
